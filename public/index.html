      👤 Civilian

      Civilians are normal Town players. They do not have a special night ability. Their goal is to work together with the Town and identify the Mafia.

      🔴 Mafia

      Mafia members work together during the night. Mafia players can communicate privately through Mafia Chat and choose players to eliminate.

      👑 Godfather

      The Godfather is an important member of the Mafia team. The Godfather works with the Mafia to eliminate the Town and help the Mafia team win.

      🔥 Grandmafia

      Grandmafia is a special Mafia role. On the first night, Grandmafia can choose a player to become Baby Mafia.

      👵 Grandma

      Grandma is a special Town role. Her abilities can affect the game according to the rules configured by the host.

      🩺 Doctor

      The Doctor can protect a player during the night. Choose a player and use the Doctor action to save them.

      🔎 Detective

      The Detective investigates players during the night. The Detective receives information that can help discover the Mafia.

      🤡 Jester

      The Jester has a special objective and does not simply try to help the Town. The Jester must follow the special win condition defined by the game.

      💕 Clupid

      Clupid can link two players together. On the first night, Clupid chooses two different players and creates a special connection between them.

      🌙 Night

      During the night, players with special abilities can perform their actions.

      ☀️ Day

      During the day, everyone can discuss the game and vote for a player they suspect.

      🎯 Your Action

      If your role has an available action, select a player and press the action button.

      💬 Public Chat

      Public Chat is visible to everyone in the game. Use it to discuss and share information.

      🔴 Mafia Chat

      Mafia Chat is private to Mafia-team players. Use it to communicate with your teammates.

      🌙 Night

      During the night, special roles can use their abilities.

      🎯 Your Action

      Select a player and choose your available action, then press CONFIRM.

      👥 Players

      The player list shows everyone currently in the game.

      💬 Public Chat

      Use the public chat to communicate with everyone.

      🔴 Mafia Chat

      Mafia members can use Mafia Chat to communicate privately with other Mafia players.

      🎭 Your Role

      Your role is shown on the right side of the screen.
