    ⚔️ MAFIA WARS

    🎭 GAME GUIDE // CLASSIFIED

    👤 CIVILIAN

    TEAM // TOWN

    Civilians are normal members of the Town. They have no night ability. Work together, share information, find the Mafia and vote them out.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: The Town wins when all Mafia-team players are eliminated.

    🔴 MAFIA

    TEAM // MAFIA

    Mafia members act together on their turn each night. They can talk privately in Mafia Chat and each Mafia member picks a target. The target with the most Mafia votes is attacked.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: The Mafia wins when living Mafia-team players are equal to or greater than living non-Mafia players.

    👑 GODFATHER

    TEAM // MAFIA

    The leader of the Mafia team. He kills with the Mafia and shows up as NOT MAFIA when the Detective investigates him.

    ☠️ DEATH EFFECT: If the Godfather dies, every living member of the Mafia team dies with him.
    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Mafia team.

    🔥 GRANDMAFIA

    TEAM // MAFIA

    🌙 NIGHT 1: Grandmafia secretly chooses one living non-Mafia player as the future Baby Mafia.

    👶 The chosen player keeps their normal role until Grandmafia dies. Then they become Baby Mafia and join the Mafia team.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Mafia team.

    👵 GRANDMA

    TEAM // TOWN

    Grandma has a deadly defense.

    ⚔️ If the Mafia attacks Grandma, the first Mafia member who voted for that attack dies instead.
    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🩺 DOCTOR

    TEAM // TOWN

    On the Doctor's turn each night, choose one living player to protect. If the Mafia attacks that player, nobody dies tonight.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🔎 DETECTIVE

    TEAM // TOWN

    On the Detective's turn, investigate one living player. You privately learn whether they are Mafia or not. Mafia, Grandmafia and Baby Mafia show as MAFIA; the Godfather shows as NOT MAFIA.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🤡 JESTER

    TEAM // NEUTRAL

    The Jester is not part of the Town or the Mafia.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: The Jester wins the moment they are eliminated for any reason: voted out, killed by the Mafia, or lost through a Cupid link.
    🎭 YOUR DEATH IS YOUR VICTORY.

    💕 CUPID

    TEAM // TOWN

    🌙 NIGHT 1: Cupid links two other players together.

    🔗 LINKED FATE: If one linked player dies, the other dies too.
    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🛡️ BODYGUARD

    TEAM // TOWN

    🌙 Each night the Bodyguard guards one player. If the Mafia attacks that player, the Bodyguard dies in their place and the target survives.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🔫 VIGILANTE

    TEAM // TOWN

    🌙 Each night the Vigilante may shoot one player, or skip. If the Vigilante kills 2 innocent players in one game, the Vigilante dies of guilt.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    ⭐ SHERIFF

    TEAM // TOWN

    🌙 Each night the Sheriff checks one player and learns SUSPICIOUS or NOT SUSPICIOUS. Mafia, framed players, the Jester and the Executioner look suspicious. The Godfather looks innocent.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🏛️ MAYOR

    TEAM // TOWN

    ☀️ The Mayor has no night action, but their vote counts as 2 votes during the day.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🚧 ESCORT

    TEAM // TOWN

    🌙 The Escort acts first each night and blocks one player. That player's ability does nothing that night.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🎭 FRAMER

    TEAM // MAFIA

    🌙 A Mafia-team member who also kills with the Mafia. Each night the Framer frames one player, who looks like Mafia to the Detective and Sheriff.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: The Mafia team wins when they equal or outnumber the Town.

    🔮 MEDIUM

    TEAM // TOWN

    🌙 At night the Medium can talk to the dead in the Dead Chat and learn useful hints.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town.

    🪓 EXECUTIONER

    TEAM // NEUTRAL

    The Executioner gets a secret target. If the target dies in any other way, the Executioner becomes a Civilian.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: The Executioner wins when the town votes their target out.

    🧠 AMNESIAC

    TEAM // NEUTRAL

    The Amnesiac starts without a role. When the first player dies, the Amnesiac takes that player's role and abilities.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the team of the role they take.

    🏆 VICTORY CONDITIONS

    🏘️ TOWN VICTORY

    All Mafia-team players are eliminated.

    🔴 MAFIA VICTORY

    Living Mafia players are equal to or greater than living non-Mafia players.

    🤡 JESTER VICTORY

    The Jester is eliminated, however it happens.

    🌙 NIGHT PHASE

    STATUS // NIGHT

    Night turns happen one at a time, in this order: Mafia → Doctor → Detective → Cupid (Cupid only on Night 1). Each turn lasts up to 30 seconds and ends early once everyone on that turn has chosen. Everyone else sees the turn screen.

    🎯 SELECT PLAYER → USE ACTION → WAIT
    🤫 KEEP YOUR ACTION SECRET.

    ☀️ DAY PHASE

    STATUS // DAY

    Everyone alive discusses what happened and votes for one player. The player with the most votes is eliminated. A tie means nobody is eliminated. Voting ends when everyone has voted or after 2 minutes.

    💬 DISCUSS • 🔍 INVESTIGATE • ⚠️ ACCUSE • 🗳️ VOTE

    👥 PLAYERS

    DATABASE // ACTIVE PLAYERS

    The Players list shows everyone in the game:

    • Living players
    • Eliminated players ☠️
    • Your own role
    • Your Mafia teammates (if you are Mafia)

    💬 PUBLIC CHAT

    CHANNEL // EVERYONE

    Visible to everyone. Only living players can write. Use it to share clues, accuse and defend.

    💬 EVERY WORD CAN REVEAL A SECRET.

    🔴 MAFIA CHAT

    CHANNEL // CLASSIFIED

    Private to living Mafia-team players. Plan your night actions here.

    🔒 CLASSIFIED CHANNEL — TOWN ACCESS DENIED.

    🎙️ VOICE CHAT

    CHANNEL // LIVE

    The host chooses who can use voice chat. At night only the active team talks (for example Mafia on the Mafia turn); during the day all living players talk together; dead players talk with each other.

    🎛️ SOUNDBOARD

    CHANNEL // SOUND EFFECTS

    Open it with the 🎛️ SOUNDBOARD button inside the Voice Chat panel, in the lobby and during the game. Pick a sound and everyone in your room hears it. The host chooses which registered players can play sounds (guests cannot). Everyone can change their own volume or mute it. There is a short cooldown between sounds, so no spamming.

    🛍️ SHOP & POINTS

    ACCOUNT // REWARDS

    Account players earn shop points for every finished game (more for a win) and spend them in Settings → Shop on character items, tags, wallpapers, sounds, logos and music. Spending shop points never lowers your rank. Guests can play but are never ranked.
