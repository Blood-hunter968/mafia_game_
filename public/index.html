    🎭 GAME GUIDE // CLASSIFIED

    👤 CIVILIAN

    TEAM // TOWN

    Civilians are normal members of the Town. They do not have a special night ability. Their goal is to work together, gather information, identify the Mafia, and eliminate the threat.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: The Town wins when all Mafia-team players are eliminated.

    🔴 MAFIA

    TEAM // MAFIA

    Mafia members work together during the night. They can communicate privately through Mafia Chat and choose players to eliminate.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Mafia wins when the number of living Mafia-team players is equal to or greater than the number of living non-Mafia players.

    👑 GODFATHER

    TEAM // MAFIA

    The Godfather is the leader of the Mafia team. He works with the Mafia to eliminate the Town and help his team achieve victory.

    ☠️ DEATH EFFECT: If the Godfather dies, every member of the Mafia team dies with him.
    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Mafia team by reaching the Mafia victory condition.

    🔥 GRANDMAFIA

    TEAM // MAFIA

    Grandmafia is a special Mafia role.

    🌙 NIGHT 1: Grandmafia can choose one player to become Baby Mafia.

    👶 Baby Mafia remains inactive until Grandmafia dies. After Grandmafia's death, Baby Mafia becomes active.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Mafia team by reaching the Mafia victory condition.

    👵 GRANDMA

    TEAM // TOWN

    Grandma has a special defensive ability.

    ⚔️ If a member of the Mafia attacks Grandma, the first Mafia attacker dies.
    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town when all Mafia-team players are eliminated.

    🩺 DOCTOR

    TEAM // TOWN

    The Doctor can protect one player during the night. Choose a player and use the Doctor action to save them.

    ⚠️ PROTECTION RULE: The Doctor cannot protect the same player repeatedly. To protect that player again, the Doctor must first protect every other player.
    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town when all Mafia-team players are eliminated.

    🔎 DETECTIVE

    TEAM // TOWN

    The Detective investigates players during the night. The Detective receives information that can help discover the Mafia.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Win with the Town when all Mafia-team players are eliminated.

    🤡 JESTER

    TEAM // NEUTRAL

    The Jester is a neutral role. The Jester is not part of the Town or Mafia.

    🏆 WIN CONDITION: The Jester wins if they are voted out by the Town OR killed by a Mafia member.
    🎭 YOUR DEATH IS YOUR VICTORY.

    💕 CUPID

    TEAM // NEUTRAL

    Cupid can link two players together.

    🌙 NIGHT 1: Cupid chooses two different players and creates a special connection between them.

    🔗 LINKED FATE: If one linked player dies, the other linked player also dies.
    🏆 WIN CONDITION: Cupid follows the special objective assigned to the Cupid role.

    🏆 VICTORY CONDITIONS

    🏘️ TOWN VICTORY

    All Mafia-team players must be eliminated.

    🔴 MAFIA VICTORY

    Living Mafia players are equal to or greater than living non-Mafia players.

    🤡 JESTER VICTORY

    Jester is voted out or killed by Mafia.
